# Changelog

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-26)
- Play the last week of daily gaps: tap any day in the strip on the title screen. Past days never count for your streak, but your best cost for each day is kept, and each day keeps its own half-built bridge.
- The daily gap opens instantly. It used to be worked out in your browser the first time you opened it, which could take most of a second.
- Fewer big steel jobs: from 28 November, a daily gap whose cheapest stock bridge is mostly steel is swapped for the next canyon in line. Every gap up to 27 November is exactly as it was.
- The shared result now ends with the full link, https://games.oddvale.com/rickety/.
- On iPhone full screen, Escape just leaves full screen (it used to open the pause menu too). Panels keep clear of the exit button in full screen on an upright phone, and a phone held sideways shows "Tap a day to play it" under the day strip.
- Tighter pars on nine levels (Hang On, Maypole, Steel Yourself, Wonky Canyon, Big Rig Bridge, Lava Lunch, Truck Stop, The Big One, Double Decker). Each new par has a known bridge that earns all three stars. Stars you already earned stay yours.

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-26)
Plays better on phones.
- Full screen on every phone, iPhone included: tap "Play full screen" under the game. It works upright or sideways, and the view re-fits when you turn the phone.
- Changed your mind mid-drag? Drag onto the toolbar (or back to the joint) and let go: no beam is built.
- Holding a finger still on a joint no longer deletes a beam (a long press used to count as a right-click on some phones).
- The hint bubble gets out of the way of the magnifier while you drag.
- Bigger menu and material buttons, pressed states on every button, and a small buzz when you place a beam, a beam snaps, or the run ends (Android).
- The daily result card and its vote fit on small phones.
- The game bars keep clear of notches and the home bar in full screen.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-25)
- Tell us how hard today's daily gap was with one tap, and see how cheap your bridge was against everyone else's today. Anonymous: nothing about you is stored.
- A new "Tell us" card under the game: thumbs up or down and a few one-tap notes, no account needed.
- A Privacy link in the footer.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-24)
First release.

- Build bridges from four materials: plank (the road), timber, steel and rope (which only pulls).
- Press Go and the Cart drives over whatever you made. Beams change colour, swell, shake and crack as they near their limit, then snap into swinging halves.
- 24 campaign levels in three chapters: trusses, rocks to lean on, slopes, rope bridges, steel, the heavy Truck, convoys, traffic from both sides, a jump and a double-decker finale.
- Three stars a level: get across, deliver every parcel, spend par or less.
- Daily gap: a new canyon every day, the same for everyone. Cheapest crossing wins, with a streak and a result you can copy.
- Sandbox: three big maps with no budget.
- Works with mouse, touch (drag, or tap a joint and then tap where the beam ends) and keyboard.
